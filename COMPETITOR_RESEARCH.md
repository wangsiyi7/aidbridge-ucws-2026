## Pattern

- Developer tools.
- Agent skills.
- Finance agents.
- Voice assistants.
- Travel/learning apps.

## Opportunity

AidBridge takes a less crowded path:

- Public-good crisis-adjacent workflow.
- Non-technical primary user.
- Clear UI and immediate demo.
- Human-in-the-loop safety instead of autonomous overreach.
- Evidence ledger that makes the output auditable.
- Privacy redaction and resource matching, which make the output operational instead of merely conversational.
- Impact estimation and benchmark evidence, which help judges see real outcome rather than only demo polish.

This gives the project a strong story for community voters, automated repo evaluation, and expert judges.
