# AidBridge Pitch Deck

## Slide 1: AidBridge

Claim: A chaotic cry for help should become a safe, auditable community response in minutes.

Proof object: Product screenshot with three proof rails: risk, resources, field pack.

Speaker line: AidBridge is a multilingual community-aid triage app for frontline volunteers, school counsellors, NGOs, and mutual-aid teams.

## Slide 2: The First Minutes Are Broken

Claim: The highest-risk part of community aid is often the first messy message.

Proof object: Four-step failure chain.

- Incomplete WhatsApp or hotline request.
- Volunteer asks inconsistent questions.
- Evidence is lost or overshared.
- Handoff happens too late or to the wrong resource.

Speaker line: We are not replacing professionals. We are making the first human handoff safer.

## Slide 3: Product Workflow

Claim: AidBridge turns one messy message into a field-ready action pack.

Proof object: Intake -> detect needs -> score risk -> redact -> match resources -> export field pack.

Key outputs:

- First response
- Risk/confidence
- Detected needs
- Redacted brief
- Trusted resource matches
- Follow-up clock
- Evidence ledger
- Human handoff script

## Slide 4: Why This Is Different

Claim: Most public UCWS projects cluster around agents, skills, finance, and voice demos; AidBridge is a user-facing public-good workflow.

Proof object: Competitor map.

- Agent frameworks
- Developer skills
- Finance analysis
- Voice assistants
- Travel/learning apps
- AidBridge: frontline community response infrastructure

Speaker line: This is not another chatbot wrapper. It is operational infrastructure for care.

## Slide 5: Why It Scores

Claim: AidBridge is built around the official UCWS evaluation weights.

Proof object: 30/30/40 scoring bridge.

- Community Vote 30%: easy demo, emotionally clear, shareable.
- AI Evaluation 30%: clean repo, deterministic engine, tests, screenshots.
- Expert Judges 40%: real-world stakes, privacy safeguards, global scaling path.

## Slide 6: What We Submit

Claim: The project is ready for Project Wall with a clear upgrade path.

Proof object: Submission checklist.

- Static app
- README
- Tests
- Project Wall fields
- Screenshots
- Deployment instructions
- Submission audit
- Roadmap: LLM extraction, signed resource verification dates, multilingual templates, case queues

Speaker close: AidBridge wins because it makes AI useful at the exact moment when a human helper cannot afford to improvise.
